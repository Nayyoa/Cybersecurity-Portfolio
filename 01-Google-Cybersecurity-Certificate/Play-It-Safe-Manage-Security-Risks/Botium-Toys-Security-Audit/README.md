
# Botium Toys Internal Security Audit

**Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate**  
**Course 2: Play It Safe: Manage Security Risks**

## Project Overview

This project presents an internal cybersecurity audit conducted as part of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate.

The assessment focuses on Botium Toys, a fictional retail company operating both physical and online stores.

The objective was to evaluate the organization's existing security controls, identify gaps in its security posture, review compliance-related practices, and recommend improvements.

## Objectives

- Review the organization's audit scope and risk assessment.
- Identify existing and missing security controls.
- Evaluate compliance-related practices against PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC 1/SOC 2 criteria.
- Assess risks to sensitive information and business operations.
- Develop prioritized security recommendations.

## Audit Scope

The assessment covered the organization's security program, including:

- Internal networks and IT infrastructure
- Employee devices and workstations
- Business applications and databases
- Customer information and payment card data
- Access controls and authentication
- Legacy systems
- Physical security controls
- Data protection and business continuity

## Risk Assessment

**Initial risk score: 8/10 (High)**

The provided assessment identified several security weaknesses:

- Excessive employee access to sensitive information
- Lack of encryption for payment card data
- Missing least privilege and separation of duties controls
- No intrusion detection system
- No critical data backups or disaster recovery plan
- Weak password requirements
- Incomplete asset inventory and classification

These weaknesses increase the organization's exposure to unauthorized access, data breaches, operational disruption, and regulatory penalties.

## Assessment Results

| Assessment area | Results |
|---|---|
| Security controls | 7 present / 7 missing |
| PCI DSS practices | 0 of 4 satisfied |
| GDPR practices | 2 of 4 satisfied |
| SOC 1/SOC 2 practices | 2 of 4 satisfied |
| Overall compliance checklist | 4 of 12 satisfied |

## Key Recommendations

1. **Strengthen access controls:** Implement least privilege, role-based access controls, and separation of duties.
2. **Protect sensitive information:** Encrypt customer and payment card data.
3. **Improve business continuity:** Establish regular backups and a disaster recovery plan.
4. **Strengthen authentication:** Update password policies and introduce centralized password management.
5. **Improve threat detection:** Deploy an intrusion detection system.
6. **Improve asset management:** Maintain an asset inventory and establish scheduled legacy-system maintenance.
